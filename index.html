Profile

Methods, instruments, and the data they produce

I am completing a Ph.D. in analytical chemistry at the Georgia Institute of Technology, where I designed and built an aqueous-in-gas microfluidic droplet system from concept through commissioning — learning fabrication, fluid dynamics, and high-speed imaging analysis along the way.

Before that I trained as a laboratory technologist in Ghana, building electronic instrumentation and running teaching laboratories. My published work spans method development for pharmaceutical extractables, high-throughput lipid analysis by supercritical fluid chromatography, and non-targeted screening of food-contact materials.

I am looking for analytical development, R&D, applications, or process development roles in analytical instrumentation, pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, chemical manufacturing, or advanced materials.
